# Visual Design System

## Source-derived brand direction
The PowerPoint (slide 26) specifies:
- Forest green: trust / primary brand colour
- Warm ivory: natural background
- Charcoal: text and contrast
- Muted gold: restrained gifting accent
- Serif for brand name and headings; clean sans-serif for facts panels
- Kraft or matte-ivory stand-up pouch with window and facts strip
- Rigid gift box, Jaipur block-print sleeve, brass-tone foil, handwritten-style note card
- Product photography on stone and wood, daylight, packer's hands

## Master visual language
- Premium-affordable, trustworthy, clean, editorial.
- Natural daylight or controlled soft studio light; realistic food texture.
- Stone, warm wood, matte ivory and restrained Jaipur craft cues.
- Avoid over-propping, excessive saturation, fake labels, visible competitor styling and generic stock-photo aesthetics.
- Keep product shape, grade and ingredient composition accurate.
- Use negative space for web headlines; never bake copy, price, logo or CTA into images.

## Ratios (production recommendations)
- Homepage desktop hero: 16:9 or 3:2 depending on layout; keep copy-safe area.
- Mobile hero: dedicated 4:5 or 9:16 composition.
- Product packshot: 1:1.
- Product detail/lifestyle: 4:5.
- Editorial/story banners: 16:9.
- Category tiles: 1:1 or 4:5.
