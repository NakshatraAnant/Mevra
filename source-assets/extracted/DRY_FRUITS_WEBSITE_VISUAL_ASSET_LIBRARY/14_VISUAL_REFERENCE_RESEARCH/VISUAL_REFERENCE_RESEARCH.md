# Visual Reference Research

Research conducted 25 November 2026. These are reference observations only; no competitor imagery is included in the deliverable.

## Design implications for this project
1. Preserve the presentation's own proposition: “Graded. Dated. Honest.”
2. Keep the palette to forest green, warm ivory, charcoal and restrained antique gold.
3. Give each SKU a consistent main/detail/lifestyle/flat-lay/scale/editorial image system.
4. Use separate gifting compositions; do not force all product imagery into a generic snack aesthetic.
5. Keep B2B imagery professional and ingredient-led rather than commodity-wholesale.
6. Avoid claims, certificates, QR destinations, dates, lot IDs, legal numbers or logos that have not been supplied and verified.
