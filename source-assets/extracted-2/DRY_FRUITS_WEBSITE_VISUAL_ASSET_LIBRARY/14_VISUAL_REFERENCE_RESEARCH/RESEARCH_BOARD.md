# Visual reference research board

**Scope and honesty note.** This is a written reference board, not an image collage. No competitor or stock photographs are included in this ZIP: they are copyrighted, and the brief forbids using them as assets. Research was desk-level (web search on e-commerce image standards and premium food-photography practice, plus the deck's own competitive benchmarks: Farmley, Happilo, Nutraj, Vedaka, Tata Sampann). The founder or art director should still browse those brands' live sites before the shoot / generation run, using the checklist at the end.

## What the category does (and where we differ)

| Observation in the Indian D2C dry-fruit category | Implication for our visual language |
|---|---|
| Funded brands (Farmley, Happilo) lean on bright, saturated colour, playful snack-brand energy and flavoured products | We look like a *trusted house*, not a snack startup (deck slide 26): ivory, forest green, sandstone, daylight, restraint |
| Traditional players (Nutraj) show product in jars/pouches on white with heavy pack graphics | Our pack front stays clean; proof (grade, date, lot) is carried by a facts strip rendered in code, not baked into photos |
| Category heroes often use floating/splashing nuts | Rejected: grounded product on real surfaces, hands of the packer (slide 26/30), natural shadows |
| Gift pages often look generic (red/gold Diwali clip-art) | Restrained Jaipur craft: block-print sleeve, brass-tone foil, haveli stone, marigold used sparingly |

## Principles borrowed from e-commerce image practice

- **Gallery #1 must identify the product at thumbnail size** on a clean background, consistent across the grid; styled images come after. (Main image = pouch + heap on ivory.)
- **Food galleries work best as a repeatable set of angles**: hero/pack, macro texture, scale (on palm), in-use/lifestyle, flat-lay. Our 6-view system follows this.
- **Surfaces and props must match price point** (stone, linen, restrained colour for premium) and avoid clutter.
- **Product images are square** (Shopify themes display product media 1:1; 2048x2048 is the common recommendation).
- **Hero images need a separate mobile composition** (4:5 portrait) because wide banners crop badly on phones.

## Reference checklist for the live-site review (before generation)

For each brand homepage, PDP and gifting page, note: hero subject & where copy sits; how many gallery images and which angles; whether texture macros are used; how gift boxes are shot (open vs closed, hands, setting); mobile hero crop; what feels generic. Record one sentence of *why it works*, never a screenshot to imitate.
